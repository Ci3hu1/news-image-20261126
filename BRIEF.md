## Visual language — "BLUEPRINT ATLAS" (own recipe)

NOT the cream newspaper front page the field copies, NOT NI24's navy
night-desk atlas, NOT NI25's daylight wire desk. A blueprint sheet:
deep blueprint-blue ground (#0E2A5C → #123C7E), faint white grid,
white ink, amber (#FFB000) signal chips, cyan (#7FD4FF) for CONFIRMED
flags and map dots. Masthead in a white double rule; two big hero
panels with amber number chips; a schematic dot-map of the world with
numbered pins; an 8-region count bar; a 6-event UTC timeline; 30
stories in 2 columns; a DATE CHECK box that states the coverage window
and agrees with the masthead; sources per story (Reuters / AP / AFP
wires only).

## 300px legibility gate

Masthead, both hero headlines, region counts and the DATE CHECK must
read at 300px wide. Grid headlines are 17px bold white on blueprint —
checked in preview-300.png; any headline the renderer truncates is
shortened in the data, not left with "…".

## Files

- render_poster.py — draws entry.png from the STORIES table in this
  file's data (pure Pillow; DejaVu fonts from the system).
- main.py — pure stdlib: copies bundled entry.png to
  $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png (else /output/entry.png), chmod 0644.
- entry.png — the rendered poster (1024×1024, ≤1 MiB).
- preview-300.png — the 300px legibility render.
- tests/ — determinism, size, clearance, truncation, story-count tests.
- STRATEGY.md, README.md, NOTES.md (added after build).
